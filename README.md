# crm-big-data-analytics-graduation-project# Evaluating the Use of Big Data Analytics Techniques to Improve CRM and Guide Strategic Decisions in Jordanian Companies

Bachelor's graduation project, Business Information Technology, Jadara University (2026).
Supervisor: Dr. Khaldoun Al-Jahmani

## Overview
Many Jordanian companies hold large amounts of data but struggle to turn it into value. This study evaluates how the use of big data analytics techniques affects Customer Relationship Management (CRM) and strategic decision-making.

## Hypotheses
1. Big data analytics has a positive effect on CRM.
2. Big data analytics has a positive effect on strategic decisions.
3. Better CRM has a positive effect on strategic decisions.

## Methodology
- Descriptive-analytical field study (pilot, cross-sectional)
- Questionnaire: 30 items, 5-point Likert scale, 3 axes
  (Big data analytics: 12, CRM: 10, Strategic decisions: 8)
- Sample: 35 employees from telecom, banking, insurance, industry, and e-commerce/services companies
- Analysis: descriptive statistics, Pearson correlation, simple linear regression (α = 0.05)
- Reliability: Cronbach's alpha = 0.915 overall (0.855 / 0.802 / 0.792 per axis)

## Key Findings
| Axis | Mean (1-5) |
|---|---|
| Big data analytics | 3.83 |
| CRM | 3.77 |
| Strategic decisions | 3.80 |

| Relationship | Beta | R² | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Big data analytics → CRM | 0.631 | 0.500 | Significant |
| Big data analytics → Strategic decisions | 0.518 | 0.345 | Significant |
| CRM → Strategic decisions | 0.523 | 0.280 | Significant |

Top barriers reported: shortage of analytical skills (3.91), data scattered across systems (3.88), weak data governance (3.84).

## Conclusion & Recommendations
The value of big data analytics comes from data quality, integration, management support, analytical skills, and an evidence-based decision culture, not from simply having data. Recommended: strengthen data governance, connect CRM systems to analytics platforms, and invest in analytics training.

## Limitations
Small pilot sample (n = 35), self-reported responses, no cross-sector comparison.
